Who We Are

Engineering Solutions Built for Kenya's Toughest Roads

Capital One Motors is a specialist truck body fabrication company based in Thika, serving logistics, transport and construction businesses across Kenya. We don't just repair vehicles, we engineer commercial vehicle solutions built for performance, durability and demanding industries.

Every project passes through precision MIG and ARC welding, structural fabrication and automotive-grade spray painting, handled by technicians who treat every joint and every panel as a reflection of our name.

  • Workshop based at Munene Industries, Muindi Mbingu Street, Thika
  • Every build finished to an automotive-grade paint standard
  • Strict quality control at every stage, no shortcuts taken
