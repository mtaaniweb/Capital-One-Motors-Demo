About Capital One Motors

More Than Fabrication. We Build Reliable Solutions.

Capital One Motors Ltd is a specialist automotive fabrication company based in Thika, Kenya. Every vehicle has a purpose, and every business depends on reliability.

We design and manufacture custom truck bodies, pickup cargo solutions, chassis builds and automotive fabrication work built around real working conditions, not showroom conditions.

Quality is not only seen in the final product. It is built into every step.
